Privacy Policy — Auto Publisher

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Auto Publisher is a private automation tool used solely by its owner to publish video content to TikTok. We take your privacy seriously.

2. Data We Collect

The app accesses:

  • TikTok account credentials (OAuth access token) to publish content on your behalf
  • Video files stored in Google Drive for processing and upload

3. How We Use Your Data

All data is used exclusively to automate video publishing. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's private server. Video files are processed and deleted after upload.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with:

  • Google Drive API (Google LLC)
  • TikTok Content Posting API (ByteDance Ltd)

Their respective privacy policies apply to data handled by their platforms.
